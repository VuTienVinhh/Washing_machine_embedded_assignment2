control behavior, not a photograph of an assembled machine.

\subsection{What Still Needs a Physical Check}
The physical circuit has not been assembled or tested in this work. If a board
demonstration is required, upload the compiled sketch and check the LEDs,
controls and timeout. Record the board and Serial Monitor
together. The software tests do not measure real switch bounce, wiring quality,
coin acceptor signals or a motor driver.
