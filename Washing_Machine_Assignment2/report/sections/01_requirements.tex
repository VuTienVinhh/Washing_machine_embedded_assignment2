\section{Problem and Requirements}
The project controls a washing machine with coins, three buttons and two LEDs.
A customer pays at least 50 cents and presses RUN to start a 30 minute cycle.
PAUSE stops washing while the timer continues. RUN resumes the same cycle.
The controller returns to standby after the time ends or after two STOP presses.
These rules come from the assignment sheet~\cite{brief}.

The implementation covers the control unit. Washing is represented by a digital
enable signal. Water valves, drum speed and wash stages are outside the given task.

\subsection{Required Behavior}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}p{0.85cm}Y@{}}
\toprule
ID & Requirement\\
\midrule
R1 & Use STOP, RUN and PAUSE as the three machine controls.\\
R2 & Accept only 10, 20 and 50 cent coins. Add their values to the credit.\\
R3 & Keep RLED on in standby. Make RLED blink when an error occurs.\\
R4 & Turn BLED on when credit reaches at least 50 cents.\\
R5 & Start only after enough credit and a RUN press. Clear all credit, including surplus.\\
R6 & Start a 30 minute timer on the first RUN. Make BLED blink while washing.\\
R7 & On PAUSE, stop washing but keep the timer counting. RUN resumes without restarting it.\\
R8 & End at 30 minutes, including paused time, or after two separate STOP presses.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\subsection{Choices for Details Not Given in the Brief}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}p{3.15cm}Y@{}}
\toprule
Detail & Choice used in this project\\
\midrule
Ready indication & Ready is still available before a cycle, so RLED stays on and BLED is on.\\
Paused indication & RLED is off, BLED stays on and the wash output is off.\\
Two STOP presses & Count two separate press events in one active cycle. No time window is imposed. PAUSE and RUN keep the count.\\
Active cycle coins & Ignore coins in Running, Paused and Error. The coin input is a bench emulator of a validated acceptor.\\
Error and recovery & A test fault signal enters Error. Washing stops and credit clears. Clear the fault and reset the board to recover.\\
Blink and buttons & Use a 1 second blink period and 30 ms button debounce. A held button gives one event.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

operation has not been measured. The supplied brief does not specify a board or
