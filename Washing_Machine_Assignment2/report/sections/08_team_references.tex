\section{Team Work and Review Plan}
\subsection{Proposed Work Split}
The following split gives each member a clear output. Each person should explain
their part and review one other part before submission.

\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}p{4.55cm}Y@{}}
\toprule
Member & Responsibility and output\\
\midrule
\studentA\newline\idA & Requirements, explicit assumptions, module diagram, state diagram and transition table. Review the code against the rules.\\
\studentB\newline\idB & C++ controller, button debounce, board sketch and pin mapping. Review the simulator's timer, outputs and STOP behavior.\\
\studentC\newline\idC & Browser demo, test evidence, report assembly and PDF checks. Review the state table and all submission files.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\subsection{Schedule with Two Review Days}
Use a seven day plan only if seven days are available; the brief supplied here
does not give a deadline. Reserve the last two days for review.

\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}p{2.0cm}Y@{}}
\toprule
Period & Work and completion check\\
\midrule
Days 1 and 2 & Agree on requirements and state rules. Finish diagrams and a first working controller.\\
Days 3 and 4 & Complete the sketch and simulator. Run coin, pause, timeout, STOP and error tests.\\
Day 5 & Merge all files. Finish the report and freeze new features.\\
Day 6 & Review each other's parts. Repeat failed tests and check any required board demonstration.\\
Day 7 & Check names, IDs, figures, contents, source package and submission rules. Submit the final files.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\vspace{6pt}
The controller implements the coin threshold, timed cycle, pause and resume,
two press stop and error indication. The reported results are software tests.
A physical demonstration, if required separately, remains a group check.
